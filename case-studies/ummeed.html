Lessons from Ummeed: Seeing the whole child across clinic and training data

Ummeed joined its clinic and training systems so clinicians, managers and fundraisers can see each child's full journey, refreshed every week.

Partner
Ummeed Child Development Center, Mumbai
Work
Children with developmental disabilities and their families; 15,500 children, 152,000 sessions, 11,200 professionals trained
Tools
Clinic Management System, Training Management System, Superset
With Dalgo since
Dasra's Data Catalyst Program, run with Dalgo

Key results

  • Monthly → weeklyData refresh, with no manual cleaning in between
  • 2 systems, 1 viewClinic (15,500+ records) and training (14,000+) joined into each child's journey
  • 4 teamsM&E, program, senior management and fundraising use the same dashboards

The challenge

A child might first come to Ummeed's clinic at 2 or 3 and return at 7 or 8, while their caregivers attend trainings and the family uses the Family Resource Center. Each program kept its own system, and the rest lived in Excel.

  • Monthly manual exports. Cleaning an MIS Excel dump took the M&E team a couple of days, so analysis ran once a month.
  • Reports nobody could explore. Power BI ran on static Excel with no licence, so reports went out as PDFs.
  • Data stayed with managers. Program teams rarely saw the numbers.

A 2022 Family Outcome Survey scored families 2.9 out of 7 on awareness of their rights. Acting on that needed a view of which services each family had used, and no system could show it.

The fix

Through Dasra's Data Catalyst Program, the team first traced children's journeys by hand, matching Excel records by phone number. It worked, and it showed the job needed a system.

  • Both systems connected. CMS and TMS data arrive weekly through their APIs, cleaned and joined automatically.
  • Three dashboards. Clinic, Training, and Pathways, which follows each child across both.
  • A team that runs it. A 4-day handover training on Superset, access roles, data quality checks and the dbt models.

The transformation

AreaBefore DalgoAfter Dalgo
Where data livesCMS, TMS and Excel, separatelyOne warehouse, joined by child
UpdatesOnce a month, by handEvery week, automatically
ReportsStatic PDFsLive dashboards with reporting-period filters
Who uses the dataMid-level and senior managersM&E, program, senior management, fundraising
TeamWhat it decides with the data now
Clinicians and care teamSee a child's sessions, caregiver trainings and other services before the consultation
Program managersRead clinic and training numbers together, so a month with fewer sessions and more trainings reads correctly
M&ESpends its time on teams' new questions instead of cleaning
FundraisingPulls ready charts into donor reports; dashboards for donors themselves are not built yet

What’s next

Teams now ask M&E for more, which Vinodhini calls a cyclic loop. Next, Ummeed plans to model how families move through its services, link the journey to child development outcomes, and bring in its other programs. Her advice to other NGOs: do your homework on what you want before you start.
