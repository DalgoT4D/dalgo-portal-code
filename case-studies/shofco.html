SHOFCO: A live view of program data for every team

SHOFCO worked with the Dalgo team to bring four tools and paper into one pipeline. Leadership, MEL teams, caseworkers and donors now each decide from the view they need.

Partner
Shining Hope for Communities (SHOFCO), Kenya
Programs
Gender, WASH, Livelihoods, Education; 2.4 million+ people in 30+ counties
Tools
CommCare, Google Sheets, Mobiwater, Superset
Engagement
Platform plus consulting since October 2024, with 40+ hours of training

Key results

  • Every team, one live viewLeaders check reported numbers against the dashboards in review meetings
  • 3 times a dayData refresh, from CommCare to dashboards
  • Months → daysTime to roll out a new dashboard
  • 12 hrs → 2 hrs a weekData cleaning time in the Gender team

The challenge

SHOFCO started in Kibera in 2004 and now works in over 30 counties. Each program collects its own data: GBV cases and counselling in Gender, attendance in Education, water dispensed in WASH, income in Livelihoods. That data sat in CommCare, Google Sheets, the Mobiwater system and on paper.

  • Manual cleaning every cycle. Weekly internal reports and monthly-to-bi-annual donor reports each meant downloading, cleaning and merging by hand: 4+ hours a week for caseworkers, 8+ for the MEL team.
  • Static reports. PDFs and slides. Every follow-up question went back to the MEL team.
  • A 2023 stack that did not fit. CommCare did not suit every program, the setup needed outside experts, and Power BI licences ruled out field-level dashboards.

How we worked together

  • Designed with each team. The Dalgo team asked caseworkers, the MEL team and leadership which questions they needed answered, then built KPIs for leadership, program tracking for MEL, donor views, and caseload views where each caseworker sees only their own cases.
  • One pipeline behind it. CommCare, Google Sheets and the Mobiwater API flow into one store, cleaned on arrival, refreshed three times a day. Programs kept the tools that suit them.
  • Skills that stay. Training on dashboards, data models and pipeline troubleshooting, so SHOFCO's own team handles new requests. Adoption held because the dashboards fit caseworkers' routines and caseworkers co-designed features.

The transformation

Every team now works from the same live data, and each makes a different decision with it.

Leadership. Reviews used to wait for end-of-week or end-of-month reports. Now leaders check reported numbers against the dashboards in review meetings and question gaps on the spot.

Program managers. They once reported a single total of participants reached. Now they check each caseworker's progress against ROC club targets.

MEL team. Data cleaning in the Gender team fell from 12 hours to 2 hours a week, and that time now goes to validating data. The team spots problems before they grow, such as gaps by gender on a mental-health indicator.

Caseworkers. They moved from memory, sticky notes and wall charts to their own caseload, tracked digitally. Each month they track open, resolved and closed cases and follow up on the ones that stay open, so no survivor falls through the cracks.

What’s next

Dashboards are live for Gender, WASH, Livelihoods and Education. The SHOFCO Urban Network, libraries and savings cooperative come onto Dalgo next.
