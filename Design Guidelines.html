07 — Principles

Do & Don't

Do

  • One primary + one secondary per section; primary leads.
  • Keep the green for actions, highlights, and eyebrows only.
  • Use the pill radius for every button; 44px minimum tap height.
  • Pair labels with their approved destinations above.
  • Reserve dark bands for one closing CTA moment per page.

Don't

  • Stack three+ competing buttons in one view.
  • Invent new greens, gradients, or shadow depths.
  • Use square corners on actions or full-saturation fills on secondary.
  • Write new CTA copy — reuse the approved labels.
  • Turn body text green except approved inline links.
