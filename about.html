Why We Exist

The idea behind Dalgo

Why we exist, who makes it possible, and how we deliver — the purpose, the people, and the platform behind the work.

Why we exist

Better data creates better decisions.

Every nonprofit deserves data systems that help them focus on impact instead of spreadsheets. Good data work should give teams their time back for the mission.

Who makes it possible

Open knowledge. Shared ownership.

Dalgo is built alongside the social sector — with the M&E leads, field teams, and directors who use it every day. Researchers, nonprofits, engineers, and practitioners continuously shape the platform through collaboration, transparency, and shared learning.

How we deliver

Data infrastructure, handled for you.

Open source, DPDP compliant, and impact focused — designed around your own data warehouse, Dalgo handles the difficult parts of working with data so teams can focus on learning, reporting, and improving programmes.

Meet the team

Dalgo is built and run by a small, dedicated team alongside the nonprofits we serve — spanning product, consulting, data engineering, and community. We also work with local partners to design and deliver our programs. Meet the people powering the work.
