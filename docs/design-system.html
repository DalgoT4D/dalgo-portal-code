Spacing

An 8pt scale, plus exactly three section-padding values. Section padding is not a free choice: white sections, tinted or dark chapter bands, and slim strips — nothing else. Two white sections meeting each pay full padding, which is why the follower usually zeroes its top.

Radius

Radius scales with the element. Pills for buttons and chips; larger radii as the surface grows.

Elevation

A layered navy-tinted ramp with the 1px hairline ring baked into the last layer, so cards carry no separate border. --shadow-md and --shadow-lg are aliases onto this ramp, kept so older usages lift with it.

Section backgrounds

Three, and no more. --surface-2 is component-only — inputs, chips, thumbnails — and never a section background. Two tinted sections never sit adjacent, and a page spends at most one or two dark bands.

Standing rules

These are not preferences. Each one exists because breaking it shipped a defect.

✕
Never underline or decorate a heading accent. .hl-underline, .cvh-hl, .pf-title-hl and every headline highlight are teal colour only — no underline, squiggle, border or ::after.
✕
A section description never exceeds 2 rendered lines. A third line is a failure, not a nitpick. Fix it by widening the container, never by editing the copy. Gated by scripts/check-copy-length.mjs inside npm run build.
✕
No copy changes without Stuti's explicit go-ahead — including to satisfy a design rule. Propose and wait.
✕
Zero !important in app.css. Solve specificity with selector scope. Exactly two stylesheets ship: tokens.css (variables only, one :root) and app.css.
✕
No play/pause controls in this UI, and no teal body text (links excepted).
✓
One quote-card pattern for every testimonial: white card, --r-xl, --shadow-card, quote ≤28px at weight 500, one mint <mark> highlight instead of bold. No naked pull-quotes.
✓
One hero visual per page — one image, no collages. A hero shows a product illustration or a photo of the people it describes, never another page's customer photo.
✓
Verify every asset against its background. Measure per-asset luminance; never eyeball one and assume the rest. A pale wordmark needs brightness(0); a solid emblem needs grayscale.
✓
QA at section boundaries, in full-page context, across ≥2 viewports. Screenshot the seams — token conformance alone does not catch a collision.
